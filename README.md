**Sunset Keys** is a 3D third-person open-world crime game set in Solano Bay, a sun-soaked coastal city of my own invention. Steal cars, outrun Bay Patrol, and work through 8 staged story jobs plus a street-race grand prix (9 jobs total) with your crew — Mira the driver, Lio the mechanic, and Kiki the lookout — to collect every Sunset Key and take the city. It has traffic and pedestrians, a 0–5 wanted level with chasing cruisers, roadblocks and an Air One helicopter with a spotlight, 7 weapon slots, crew abilities (plate swap, quick fix, eyes out), respect ranks, money, a garage, synthesized radio stations, saves, and a full HUD with a circular minimap, mission banner, dispatch panel and dialogue. Built with vendored three.js and procedural geometry only — no Rockstar names, art, music or maps, and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to drive and walk, E to steal a car, F to shoot, Q / 1–7 for weapons, Z/X/C for crew abilities, R for radio, G at the garage to repair, P to pause.
